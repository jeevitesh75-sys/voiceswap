VoiceSwap Terms

VoiceSwap changes the sound of a voice recording. Please use it fairly.

  • Only use a voice that is yours, or that the person has clearly allowed you to use.
  • Do not copy the voice of a real person (for example an actor, a singer, a politician or someone you know) without their permission.
  • Do not use it to trick, scam, threaten, bully or harm anyone, or to pretend to be someone you are not.
  • Tell people when a voice is AI-changed. Files made here carry an "AI voice" label unless you turn it off, and you are still responsible for how you use them.
  • When you save a voice you tick a permission box. The time of the tick is stored with the voice.
  • Results are deleted automatically after a day. Saved voices stay until you delete them.
  • The owner may remove voices or block users who break these rules.
