How to use Share from WhatsApp
  1. Install VoiceSwap: Chrome menu → Add to Home screen → Install.
  2. In WhatsApp, record a voice note but do not send it. Or long-press a voice note you already have.
  3. Tap the menu (three dots) → Share → VoiceSwap.
  4. Pick a voice here, tap Change voice, then Share the result back.
If your WhatsApp has no Share option for voice notes, tap Choose an audio above instead.
